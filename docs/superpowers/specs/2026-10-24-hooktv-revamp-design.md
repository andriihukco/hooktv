# HOOK TV Revamp: Brand, Flows and One-Tap Watch

Date: 2026-10-24
Status: Draft, pending user review
Repository: `~/Desktop/CURRENT/lampa` (package `hook-tv`, a Lampa fork)

## Goal

Make HOOK TV look and behave like a top-tier streaming service, with Apple TV as the main reference and Netflix and Hulu as secondary ones. The work covers six things:

- a new brand (pill-badge logo, electric-lime accent);
- a Netflix-style intro;
- an Apple-style language picker;
- top-pill navigation with Favourites next to Search;
- flat settings;
- a Watch flow that asks only "Original or Ukrainian" and then plays the best available source at once, in an Apple-style player. Users never see balancers or source lists.

## Decisions Already Made

- **Logo:** direction #7 from the reference grid. A dark rounded pill holds "HOOK", whose second "O" is a play button in lime, and "TV" in lime. The play-O on its own is the app icon.
- **Accent:** electric lime `#C8F542`. Text on lime is near-black `#0B0F00`. The background stays black.
- **Navigation:** Apple TV top pills replace the side menu.
- **Dub prompt:** ask on every Watch, with the last choice preselected. Series reuse the title's choice for following episodes without asking again.
- **Languages:** only Original and Ukrainian are offered. Russian voices are never offered or auto-selected.
- **Escape hatch:** the existing source list (`hook_online` component) stays reachable through a small "All sources…" link. It is not removed.
- **Platforms:** HOOK TV runs in browsers, on phones and on TVs (webOS, Tizen). Every control must work with a TV remote through Lampa's `selector` + `Controller` focus system, with a touch screen, and with a mouse.
- **Out of scope:** new content sources, backend/API changes (`bbe.lme.isroot.in`), accounts, and torrent internals.

## Design

### 1. Brand

- `public/img/logo.svg`: the pill wordmark.
  - The pill is filled `#0B0B0C` with a 1px `rgba(255,255,255,.12)` hairline, so it reads on black.
  - "HOOK" is a heavy geometric sans in white, and the second O is a lime circle with a black play triangle.
  - "TV" is lime, outside the pill and to its right, exactly as in reference #7.
  - The SVG uses outlined paths, not live text, so no font is needed.
- `public/img/logo-icon.svg`: the play-O in a dark rounded square (app icon).
- `public/favicon.ico` and the app/TV icon PNGs are exported from the icon at the sizes the platform manifests already list.
- **Tokens** (`brand.js`, `brand.scss`, and `public/css/brand.css` regenerated by the build):
  - `--brand-primary: #C8F542`
  - `--brand-primary-hover: #D6FF5C`
  - `--brand-primary-dark: #A6D12A`
  - `--brand-on-primary: #0B0F00`
  - `--brand-background: #000`
  - `--surface-1: #121214`, `--surface-2: #1C1C1F`
  - `--glass: rgba(30,30,32,.62)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(30px) saturate(1.4)`
  - `--text-primary: #fff`, `--text-secondary: rgba(255,255,255,.62)`
  - `--focus-ring: #C8F542`
- **Remove:** the `#E50914` leftovers and `--netflix-red`. Every `var(--brand-primary, #E50914)` fallback becomes `var(--brand-primary)`.
- `public/index.html`: `<title>HOOK TV</title>` and `<meta name="theme-color" content="#000000">`.
- **Docs:** `BRANDING_GUIDE.md` and `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` are updated from "StreamFlow" and red to HOOK TV and lime.

**Contrast:** `#0B0F00` on `#C8F542` is ≥ 14:1, and `#C8F542` on `#000` is ≥ 14:1. The focus ring is a 3px lime ring with 2px of black space between it and the element. It is ≥ 3:1 on both dark and photo backgrounds because of the black gap.

### 2. Intro

This replaces the content of `.welcome` (`loading_progress.js` and `welcome.scss`).

- **Sequence** (about 1.8 s, CSS keyframes only):
  1. The dark pill scales from 0.92 to 1 and fades in (0–400 ms).
  2. The play-O "ignites": it fills with lime and a soft lime glow pulses once (400–900 ms).
  3. "TV" slides in from the left by 8px (700–1100 ms).
  4. The logo holds, then the whole layer crossfades to the app (1400–1800 ms).
- App loading runs in parallel. If loading is still running at 1.8 s, the logo stays and a 2px lime indeterminate line appears under it. The old status text shows only after 3 s, for diagnosis.
- **Skip:** OK, Back, Enter or a tap ends the intro early once the app is ready. The existing `app.js:498` handler is reused.
- **Reduced motion:** with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the intro is a plain 300 ms fade.
- The intro runs on every cold start. It does not run on in-app reloads.

### 3. Language picker (first run only)

This rewrites `src/templates/lang.js` and `lang.scss`. The logic in `src/interaction/lang.js` is simplified.

- One screen, with no "suggested/all" toggle.
- Layout: a blurred black backdrop (`welcome.jpg` at 20% opacity), the large title "Choose your language" in the detected language, then a centred frosted grouped list (max width 560px).
- Each row shows the language in its own name ("Українська", "English", …) and a checkmark on the selected row. The detected language is preselected and sorted first. The rest are alphabetical by native name.
- A lime "Continue" pill sits below the list.
- **Remote:** up/down moves through the list, OK selects, and the next OK on Continue proceeds.
- The existing IP geo-detection stays, with a 1.5 s timeout. After that the picker falls back to `navigator.language`.
- What gets stored is unchanged: `language` and `tmdb_lang`.

### 4. Top navigation

This rewrites `src/templates/head.js` and the head styles. The side menu (`menu.js`) is hidden and its items are rehomed.

- **Left:** the logo pill (`logo.svg`, 28px high).
- **Centre:** the pills **Home · Movies · TV Shows · Library**. The active pill is filled `rgba(255,255,255,.92)` with black text. Inactive pills are white text on transparent. A focused pill gets the lime ring.
  - Home, Movies and TV Shows map to the existing `main`, `category` (movie) and `category` (tv) routes.
  - **Library** is a new screen built from existing components. It is a grid of large tiles: Continue Watching (history), Favourites, Bookmarks/Collections, Downloads/Torrents (only if `torrents_use`), and any other item the old side menu had.
- **Right:** ⌕ Search (the existing `open--search` action), ♡ Favourites (new, via `Head.addIcon`, opening `Router.call('favorite')` behind the same `ParentalControl.personal('bookmarks')` check), notices (bell, shown only when there are notices), and the profile avatar.
  - The profile avatar opens a frosted menu: Settings, Account, Switch profile, About.
- The bar is transparent over hero art, with a gradient from black at 60% to transparent behind it. Once the page scrolls it becomes `--glass`. Its height is 72px on TV and desktop. Top padding is `env(safe-area-inset-top)`.
- **Phone width (under 720px):** the pills collapse into a bottom tab bar (Home · Movies · TV · Library). The top bar keeps the logo, search, favourites and avatar.
- **Remote:** left/right moves along the bar, and down moves into the page content. Back on Home with the bar focused uses the existing exit confirmation.

### 5. Settings: one flat screen

This rewrites the `settings/main.js` and `component.js` presentation. The settings API, params and stored keys are unchanged.

- Settings opens as a full-height frosted panel on the right, 640px wide on TV and desktop and full-screen on phones. The page behind it is dimmed.
- Every section is visible on one scrolling page, as Apple-style inset grouped lists with section headers:
  1. **Interface:** language, appearance options, start page.
  2. **Playback:** default quality, autoplay next episode, skip intro, subtitle size.
  3. **Audio & dub:** the default dub (Original / Українська), which is the stored last choice, and "Always ask" (on).
  4. **Account.**
  5. **Advanced:** parser, TorrServer, TMDB, plugins, input and other rarely used folders, shown inline.
- Rows show their value on the right (for example "1080p ›"). Picking a value opens a small frosted popover list next to the row. It never opens a new screen.
- Toggles are inline lime switches.
- **Search inside settings:** a field at the top of the panel filters rows by title.
- Back closes the panel.

### 6. Details screen

This restyles `src/templates/full/start_new.js` and `full` scss, and changes `start/buttons.js`.

- **Hero:** the backdrop is full-bleed at 100% width and about 75vh, with a left-to-right black gradient (85% to 0) and a bottom fade to black.
- **Title:** the TMDB title logo when available. It is fetched from `/movie|tv/{id}/images` with `include_image_language={lang},en,null`, preferring the UI language, then English. Max 420×160. Without a logo it falls back to a 56px bold title.

### 7. Watch flow and best-source resolver

**New module:** `src/services/resolver.js`. It is pure logic, has no UI, and is exported for tests.

- `resolveCandidates(movie, { signal }) → Promise<{ original: Candidate[], ukrainian: Candidate[] }>`.
  - It uses the `APIClient` and `createV2` logic extracted from `online.js` into `src/services/online/api.js`. `online.js` keeps working by importing from there; its behaviour is unchanged.
  - It runs `getSources` then `search` across every enabled source (the user's hidden or sorted list in `SourcesStore` is respected), then `getContent` per match, all in parallel with an overall cap of 8 s.
  - Each `Candidate` is `{ sourceKey, voiceName, lang: 'uk'|'original', quality: number, ref, season?, episode? }`.
- `classifyVoice(name: string, source: string): 'uk'|'original'|'ru'|'unknown'`:
  - `uk` matches `/укр|україн|ukr|ua\b|uk\b/i`, or the source is Ukrainian-only: `uaflix`, `starlight`, `midnight`, `bambooua`.
  - `original` matches `/оригін|original|eng|англ|subs only|без перекладу/i`.
  - `ru` matches `/рус|russian|\bru\b/i`.
  - `ru` and `unknown` are never auto-selected.
- `rank(candidates)` sorts by quality (2160 > 1440 > 1080 > 720 > 480), then by source order from `SourcesStore`, then by voice name (an "official" or "дубляж" voice is preferred over "многоголос" or "одноголос").
- `playBest(candidates, opts)`:
  - It calls `getStream` on the top candidate, then `prepareStreams`, then `Player.play({ url, quality, title, timeline, subtitles })`.
  - If the stream fails or the player reports a fatal error within 10 s, it silently tries the next candidate, up to 3 attempts. After that it shows the not-available state.
- **Prefetch:** opening a details screen calls `resolveCandidates` in the background. Results are cached per `movie.id` for 10 minutes and the request is cancelled if the user leaves. Watch then usually opens with sources already known.
- **Series:**
  - Candidates are resolved per episode from the voice's seasons.
  - Watch starts the episode to continue from history, otherwise S1·E1.
  - "Next episode" reuses the chosen language and the same source and voice when possible.

**Dub sheet:** a new template and a frosted bottom sheet. On TV and desktop it is a centred card.

- Title: "Watch in". There are two large options, each with a status line:
  - **Original:** "1080p · subtitles: Українська" or "Not available".
  - **Українська:** "Дубляж · 4K" or "Not available".
- The stored last choice (`hooktv_dub`, default `uk`) is focused, so OK plays immediately.
- While candidates are still resolving, each option shows a small spinner. OK on a still-resolving option waits up to the 8 s cap with a progress shimmer.
- An unavailable option is disabled with its reason. If both are unavailable, the sheet shows "Not available right now" with Retry and "All sources…", which opens the existing `hook_online` activity.
- With "Always ask" off in Settings, Watch skips the sheet and plays the stored choice.
- Choosing an option stores `hooktv_dub` and remembers the choice per title (`hooktv_dub_by_title[id]`) for series.

### 8. Player restyle

This restyles `src/templates/player/{panel,info,footer}.js` and `src/sass/components/player/*`. `video.js` playback logic is untouched, and `panel.js` changes only where markup hooks move.

- Video is edge to edge. Controls fade after 3 s of no input, and any key, tap or mouse move brings them back.
- **Top bar:** a ✕ round glass button, the title (and "S1 · E3 — Episode name" for series), and on the right the source-quality badge ("1080p").
- **Centre:** three frosted round buttons: back 10 s, play/pause (96px), forward 10 s. On TV these sit in the remote focus row.
- **Bottom:**
  - A 4px scrubber in `rgba(255,255,255,.3)` with a lime fill. On focus or hover it grows to 8px and shows its thumb. Elapsed time is on the left and time remaining on the right.
  - Below the scrubber is a pill row: 🔊 Audio · CC Subtitles · ⚙ Quality · ⏱ Speed · ⏭ Next episode (series only).
  - Each pill opens a frosted popover list above it.
  - **Audio** lists Original and Українська. It switches live: audio tracks within the stream when present, otherwise the resolver's best candidate for the other language at the same timestamp.
- **"Next episode" card:** a glass card in the bottom right during the last 30 s or the credits (`segments.js`). It autoplays after a 10 s countdown, and OK plays it now.
- **Remote:**
  - OK toggles play/pause, and left/right seeks 10 s when the controls are hidden.
  - Up/down shows the controls and moves focus between the centre row and the pill row.
  - Back closes a popover first, then the controls, then exits the player.
- **Touch:** single tap shows or hides the controls, double tap on the left or right third seeks 10 s, and swiping down closes the player on phones.

### 9. Platform and accessibility rules (apply everywhere)

- Every interactive element has the `selector` class and registers with `Controller`. A focused element shows the lime ring.
- Hover styles go inside `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- Use `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent` and `touch-action: manipulation` on controls.
- Inputs are at least 16px font size.
- Phone layouts use `env(safe-area-inset-*)` and `100dvh`.
- Text contrast is at least 4.5:1, text over photos sits on a gradient scrim, and the minimum size on TV is 22px.
- Touch targets are at least 44px. TV focus targets are at least 64px tall.
- Motion uses `transform` and `opacity` only, with no animated `filter: blur`, because TV GPUs are weak. Glass blur is static.
- New UI strings are added to `src/lang/{uk,en,ru}.js` and to whichever other locales already exist. Ukrainian is the primary copy.

## Risks

- **Voice-name inference is heuristic.** A mislabelled voice could play the wrong dub. Mitigations: Russian is never auto-selected, the player's Audio menu switches instantly, and the classifier is tested on a fixture list of real names. Unknown names are logged in debug mode so the list can grow.
- **TMDB title logos are missing for some titles.** The design falls back to text.
- **Uncommitted work: 141 paths, about 11.7k lines.** It must be committed or branched before implementation starts. That decision is made at the plan stage.
- **The `online.js` extraction could regress the existing source UI.** Mitigation: move functions out without changing their bodies, plus the "All sources…" regression check.
- **The side menu is removed.** Every former menu item must be reachable from Library or the profile menu. The plan lists each item with its new home.
