Design system · v1

HOOK TV Design

One calm surface for films, series, live TV and games. The same tokens drive the TV remote, the phone and the desktop, so a person who learns one screen already knows the next.

Philosophy of service

HOOK TV exists so that someone can find something good to watch in a few seconds and start it. Everything on screen serves that moment. Where a choice does not help it, we remove it.

01

Content is the hero

Posters and backdrops carry the screen. Chrome stays quiet: dark surfaces, few borders, text that recedes until it is needed.

02

Built for the couch first

Assume a remote, three metres away and a glance. Every control is reachable with arrows and OK, and the focus is always visible.

03

Calm motion

Transitions are short and purposeful: 150 ms for presses, 200 ms for panels, 300 ms for pages. Motion is reduced when the person asks for less.

04

Ukrainian first, every language honest

Ukrainian is the home language, and the interface is available in 40 languages. Untranslated text falls back to English, never to an empty label. Brand names stay as they are.

05

Accessible by default

Contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA, focus rings are kept on every control, and touch targets are at least 44 px, and language and reading order are declared on every page.

06

One system, every screen

TV, phone, tablet and desktop share the same tokens and components. Layout adapts; meaning does not change.

Colour

Black is the canvas. Electric lime is the single accent for actions and focus. Ratios are computed against the surface they sit on (WCAG 2.2 contrast formula).

Components

Each component states its job and the states it must show: default, focused, pressed and disabled. The focus ring is the one state that is never removed.

Buttons

Primary: one per screen. Secondary and ghost: everything else. Minimum 44 × 44 px.

Focus

3 px lime ring with a 2 px black gap. Contrast 16.6:1 against black, so it passes WCAG 2.2 non-text contrast (1.4.11).

How this page stays true

  • Tokens are defined once, in src/sass/brand.scss. When a token changes, change it here too.
  • Every new game needs a title and hint in all 40 language packs. A test fails when one is missing or still English.
  • Every change to a component or a token is checked for contrast, focus and reduced motion before it ships.
